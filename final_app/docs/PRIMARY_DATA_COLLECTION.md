# Primary data collection protocol (lecturer feedback)

Do not present fictional demo input as field results. Obtain informed permission before interviewing retailers/suppliers. Record a date, business type (anonymise if needed), evidence category, and source reference without exposing commercially confidential price lists in a public repository.

| Data | Field / source | Unit | Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| Retailer procurement frequency | baseline record | orders/week | 2–4 week purchase log or structured interview |
| Retailer quantities | baseline record | kg/order | invoices or log |
| Retailer unit price | baseline price/kg | R/kg | invoice or quotation |
| Retailer transport cost | baseline logistics | R/order and R/kg | receipt or documented estimate |
| Procurement trips and distance | baseline trips/distance | trips, km | log/route measurement |
| Supplier price tiers | offer tiers | minimum kg, R/kg | dated supplier quote |
| Supplier capacity | offer capacity | kg per offer, shared across windows in this model | supplier confirmation, including when capacity resets in practice |
| Supplier lead time | offer lead_days | days | supplier quote |
| Supplier fill rate | offer fill_rate | fraction 0–1 | history or declared estimate |
| On-time fulfilment | fulfilment | delivered/ordered, dates | dispatch/delivery records |

Collect multiple distinct realistic scenarios and retain each assumption. Suggested scenario IDs: S1 economical feasible group purchase, S2 expensive group option rejected, S3 capacity already reserved causing conflict, S4 separate versus coordinated fulfilment (including extra cold-chain handling). Use actual collected numbers when available. In your report, mark simulated vs primary, report sample size, explain limitations and do not claim achieved savings from untested scenarios.
