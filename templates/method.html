THE METHOD

How our calculations work

Seven straightforward supply-chain calculations. See calculations.py for formulas, assumptions and input validation.

Procurement decision model

1. Aggregated demand

Q = q1 + q2 + ... + qn

Add compatible retailer orders to calculate group demand.

2. Volume price tiers

P(Q) = qualifying supplier price

Use the rate for the highest quantity threshold met.

3. Total landed cost

TC = Q × P + F + Q × V + H

Include meat, fixed delivery, variable delivery and handling.

4. Landed cost/kg

LC = TC / Q

Compare suppliers on real delivered cost per kilogram.

5. Savings

S = (Old − New) / Old × 100

Compare recommended costs with a stated baseline.

6. Feasibility

Capacity ≥ Q; lead time ≤ days; fill rate ≥ minimum

Reject suppliers unable to meet order or service requirements.

7. Shared fixed costs

Share = F × (retailer kg / group kg)

Distribute fixed group delivery costs proportionally.

Demonstration assumptions: one compatible beef product, quantities in kg, money in ZAR, and illustrative supplier terms. Taxes, quality differences and geographical constraints are not modelled yet.
