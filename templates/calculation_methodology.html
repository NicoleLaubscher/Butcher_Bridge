STEP 2 · MODEL

How the decision is made.

The standalone Python model compares compatible demand with feasible supplier options using total delivered cost.

Documented calculations

1 · Compatible demand

Q = Σ qᵢ

Only matching product, specification, required date and delivery area can be combined.

2 · Volume tier

P(Q) = highest qualifying tier

Use the price threshold reached by the group quantity.

3 · Total delivered cost

TC = QP + F + QV + H

Include meat, fixed and per-kg delivery, and handling.

4 · Cost per kg

LC = TC / Q

Compare full delivered cost, rather than meat price alone.

5 · Supplier feasibility

Capacity, lead, fill, area and tier

Retain failed options with a reason; recommend only feasible suppliers.

6 · Accept or reject aggregation

Accept when group LC < individual baseline

Reject if no supplier qualifies or if grouping ties or costs more.

7 · Allocate shared delivery

Shareᵢ = F × (qᵢ / Q)

Allocate a shared fixed fee in proportion to retailer kg.

8–10 · Measure results

Fill · On-time · OTIF · cost variance

Compare ordered and actual delivery, timing and spend after the order.

Assumptions: fictional supplier prices and service measures, ZAR, kg, one sample product, a R98.60/kg individual baseline. These are not primary data. Validate against real retailer and supplier records before making operational claims.
